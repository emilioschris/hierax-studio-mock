        Hierax [ˈhɪə.ræks]

        hierax (ἱέραξ) is the ancient Greek word for hawk. In Greek myth, Hierax was a just and honest man who honoured Demeter, goddess of the harvest, and was blessed with plentiful crops. When famine struck a neighbouring people, he sent them his barley and wheat, and Poseidon turned him into a hawk.

        I named the studio after him: a man of the land who shared what he grew.
